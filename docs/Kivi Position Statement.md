# Kivi Product Position Statement

Kivi is a personal multilingual voice layer for people who think, create and communicate more freely than they type. It connects your voice with the context of your computer. The screen tells Kivi where to look, your speech tells it what you want, and Kivi understands the intent. Kivi Anywhere brings this layer across the apps you already use, while Kivi Mic is to voice what Gboard is to typing: an intelligent voice layer built into the microphone experiences you already use, without forcing you to change your workflow. Across apps, Kivi helps you Think, Write, Understand, Communicate and Remember, while keeping you in control. Its greatest value grows through personalization, as it learns your vocabulary, styles, shortcuts, modes and ways of working. Kivi should remain a layer, not a destination. It should never become a generic chatbot, passive dictation tool, app replacement or autonomous agent.
